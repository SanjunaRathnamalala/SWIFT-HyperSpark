The Breaking Point

In Sri Lanka, the macro-economy suffers from high LPG import costs, leaving households and commercial kitchens financially burdened. Meanwhile, an untapped energy source is rotting in our backyards: approximately 60-70% of daily solid waste in Sri Lanka is organic, biodegradable material.

Despite this, biogas adoption remains painfully low (under 5%). Current systems are perceived as unreliable, inconsistent, and demand too much manual intervention. Relying solely on LPG is expensive, but relying solely on raw biogas is unpredictable.

The SWIFT Solution

Meet the intelligent bridge between renewable energy and reliable cooking. Our system integrates a scalable biogas digester with automated fuel switching. It prioritizes your generated biogas to slash your energy bills. But the moment biogas pressure drops, our smart automated switching valve seamlessly transitions to LPG. No manual nozzle adjustments. No interrupted cooking.

Under The Hood

A breakdown of the hardware and IoT integration driving the HyperSpark system.

Smart Automated Switching

Pressure sensors monitor biogas availability in real-time. If pressure drops below 1.0-1.1 bar, solenoid valves instantly engage the LPG supply.

Advanced Filtration

An integrated iron sponge filter eliminates highly corrosive Hydrogen Sulfide (H₂S), while a moisture trap protects downstream electronics and sensor longevity.

IoT Intelligence & Safety

The MCU acts as the "brain" of the safety layer, featuring leak detection (H₂S, methane, LPG), automatic shut-off protocols, and local alarms.

Mobile Command Center

Transmit IoT data via Wi-Fi. Monitor gas levels, track financial savings, and receive remote safety alerts directly on the SWIFT mobile app.

Global Impact, Local Execution

SWIFT isn't just about saving money; it's a direct intervention for climate resilience. We are strictly aligned with United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

  • SDG 13 (Climate Action): Capturing methane before it hits the atmosphere and replacing fossil fuels.
  • SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy): Providing households with a cost-effective, decentralized renewable fuel.
